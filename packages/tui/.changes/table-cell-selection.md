- Added table-aware selection to the alternate screen: a drag that starts inside a rendered Markdown table selects whole cells instead of the painted rectangle, highlights them cell by cell, and copies their content with columns separated by tabs and rows by newlines, leaving the borders out. A cell that wraps over several lines is copied unwrapped; a drag that stays on one rendered line copies exactly the columns it covered. Double- and triple-click keep selecting words and lines.
